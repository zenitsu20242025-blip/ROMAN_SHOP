Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed. You will receive a confirmation email once your order is placed and a tracking number as soon as it ships.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive within 7–12 business days after processing. Delivery times are estimates and can be longer during holidays, peak seasons, or because of customs delays.

Where we ship

We currently ship to the United States and most European countries. If your country is not accepted at checkout, we cannot deliver there yet.

Shipping costs

Any shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

Customs, duties and taxes

Depending on your country, your order may be subject to import duties or taxes charged by your local customs authority. These charges are the buyer's responsibility unless stated otherwise at checkout.

Wrong address
