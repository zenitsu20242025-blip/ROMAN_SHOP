Returns & Refunds

Last updated: September 2026

14-day returns

You may request a return within 14 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, in original condition and packaging.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item you ordered, email us at within 14 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We will send a replacement or issue a refund, at no cost to you.

How to start a return

Email with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with instructions. Please do not send items back before we confirm, as unapproved returns cannot be processed.

Return shipping

For change-of-mind returns, the buyer pays the return shipping cost. Return shipping is free when the item is defective, damaged, or incorrect.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will refund the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping fees are non-refundable except for defective or incorrect items.

Orders that never arrive

If your order has not arrived within 30 days of shipping, contact us and we will investigate, then reship or refund.
